Support

Questions, problems or ideas for Earmark.

Email hello@earmarkreading.com. It helps to say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Earmark works fully without one, and keeps everything on your iPhone. An account lets you back up and sync your reading between devices.

How do I bring my books from Goodreads? Export your library from Goodreads as a CSV file, then in Earmark go to Profile, tap the gear, and choose Import from Goodreads.

How do I get my data out? Profile, then the gear, then Export. You get your books and your reading sessions as CSV files.

How do I delete my account? Profile, then the gear, then Delete Account. This deletes your account and everything in it, and can't be undone. If you can't open the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

A book has the wrong cover or page count. Open the book, tap Edit Book, and choose the edition you own.
